Hold for a sound in Hunt
Calls and backgrounds. Most calls run about two minutes; the same hold on the button again ends one. The same hold on a background that is on turns it off, and a call playing over it keeps going. Unmute, and going from Hunt to Practice, use the same hold. In Practice everything is a plain tap.

The sound starts
With "When I let go" you can keep a full button held and pick the moment. Slide off the button to cancel.
Practice: the silence after a call
In Practice a long call is given a phrase at a time, with the silence after each. Never shorter than one second. With Record on, the phone listens during the silence and then plays the phrase and your answer back to back. Nothing recorded is kept or sent anywhere.

Long calls in Hunt play on
"Second player" is the normal setting: the call runs over the background and keeps going with the phone locked. If starting a call ever pauses the background, choose "Call player", and keep the phone unlocked while a call runs.

"From memory" is the normal setting. Try "Direct" only if a saved background stays silent.
